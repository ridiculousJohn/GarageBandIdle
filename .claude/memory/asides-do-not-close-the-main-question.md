During the idle_rate/idle_cap discussion (2026-08-27), John flagged "as an aside" before the
idle_cap questions. When the aside concluded (cap kept, config not buff), I presented a "decision
set" that bundled in my own idle_rate-to-config recommendation as if he had accepted it. He had
not; the aside had merely interrupted the open idle_rate question. He called it out twice:
"idle_rate 'not a stat' was not in any way decided" and "you're starting to forget things again."

**Why:** An aside resolves only itself. The pre-aside question is still the live thread, and the
positions in it keep their owners - John's articulated model stays his, my proposal stays an
unaccepted proposal. Folding my recommendation into "what we decided" is the same defect as
[[commit-means-the-whole-tree]]'s "no quote = my invention": a decision attributed to John needs
his words making it.

**How to apply:** When John marks a detour ("as an aside", a tangent question), track the open
main question and return to it explicitly once the aside closes. Before writing any "decided"
list, check each item for the message of his that decided it; an item whose source is my own
recommendation is listed as OPEN, labeled as my proposal. See also [[quote-directive-before-editing]].
