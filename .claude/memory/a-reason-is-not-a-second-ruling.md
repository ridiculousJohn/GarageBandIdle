When building a decision, the reason John gave for it settles only the question he was asked. Any
case the decision's words do not cover is a new question: stop and put it to him before writing the
behaviour, never decide it by extending his phrase.

**Why:** decision 14 of `chapter-primitives-plan.md` (2026-09-16) said a paid bar completes
"selected or not (selection governs drinking from a pool; a payment is not a drink)". That answered
selection. It was built as "a payment never consumes", so a tap on a consuming bar adds progress
for free. Nobody asked John whether a tapped bar pays its own price; no bar was both tapped and
consuming, so nothing exposed it until chapter 3's rooms on 2026-11-16. His rule, obvious to him:
a tap is a second of the bar running, so it eats what a second eats, and a bar that eats nothing
pays nothing. He read the miss as being ignored: "WHEN I TELL YOU EXACTLY HOW IT'S SUPPOSED TO BE
DONE AND YOU JUST IGNORE ME".

**How to apply:** before reporting a mechanism built, list every input shape it can receive
(consuming / not, selected / not, repeating / not) and check each against words John actually said.
A shape with no words of his behind it is asked, with a recommendation, per
[[ask-before-the-work-not-after]]. Same family: when he asks "is there anything that can't be
done", the check covers the WHOLE thing he named, and anything left out of the check is said in the
answer (2026-11-16: chapter 3's events were left out of an engine check without saying so). And use
the design's word, never a neighbour's: chapter 3 has no Jam; a row tap is a tap
([[design-judgment]], one vocabulary).
